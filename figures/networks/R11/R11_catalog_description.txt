R11 -- Synthetic Delaware Basin catalog (tx_syn), UTDQuake v2
=====================================================================

WHAT IT IS
  21,000 SYNTHETIC earthquakes (not real events) in the Delaware Basin, West Texas, made to teach
  earthquake-location models to locate through many different velocity models. Every event has its own
  1D velocity model (Monte Carlo around DB1D); its P and S picks are travel times through that model plus
  pick errors. Station geometry, pick patterns and magnitudes come from the real research catalog R03
  (TexNet High Resolution Earthquake Catalog, Bureau of Economic Geology, University of Texas at Austin,
  doi:10.15781/76hj-ed46). DB1D: Lomax & Savvaidis (2019), JGR Solid Earth, doi:10.1029/2019JB017727.

  Events 21,000 | picks 890,606 (P 475,885, S 414,721) | stations 174 | origin times spread over
  2017-09-20 - 2024-03-27 (one every ~3 h; they are not real times).

HOW IT WAS MADE  (code: datasets/tx_syn/tx_syn.py in github.com/ecastillot/UTDQuake)
  1. Velocity model, one per event, drawn independently (seed 11):
     - reference: DB1D, the Delaware Basin 1D velocity model of Lomax & Savvaidis (2019), Improving
       Absolute Earthquake Location in West Texas Using Probabilistic, Proxy Ground-Truth Station
       Corrections, J. Geophys. Res. Solid Earth 124(11), 11447-11465, doi:10.1029/2019JB017727; file as
       shared by Castillo, Ushakov & Savvaidis (2026), Seismological Research Letters,
       doi:10.1785/0220250288 (vp/DB_model.csv of huggingface.co/datasets/ecastillot/CMEZ-SPHighResCatalog).
       Layers at 0, 2, 6, 10, 28, 40, 46 km below sea level, plus a DTM layer from the surface to sea level. Below 46 km: the
       last DB1D layer to 120 km, IASP91 from 210 km.
     - Vp = DB1D x (1 + g + l): g shared by every layer, uniform +-10%; l per layer, uniform +-5%
       (+-15% in the sediments, above 6 km). About +-15 % overall around DB1D.
     - Vs = Vp / (r + m): r shared, uniform 1.6-2.0; m per layer +-0.05; Vp/Vs kept within 1.6-2.0, so
       Vs is always below Vp.
     - Interfaces moved uniform +-1 km (Moho, 40 km: +-3 km); layers at least 0.5 km thick.
     - Velocity never decreases with depth below 6 km; above (sediments) a layer can be up to
       10% slower than the one above (short low-velocity zones). A model breaking a rule is redrawn.
     - Result: median Vp per layer within ~4 % of DB1D; Vp/Vs 1.60-2.00.
  2. Hypocenters:
     - 70% near R03 events: a random R03 event moved by a Gaussian offset (sd 1 km in x, y and depth);
     - 30% uniform over the R03 area (31.03-31.91 N, -104.76 - -103.07 E), depth 0-15 km;
     - depths below sea level (>= 0 km).
  3. Stations and picks: each synthetic event copies the stations and phases (P, S) of the closest R03
     event (3D distance) -- the same stations pick P and S as there -- and its magnitude (ML, copied, not
     measured).
  4. Travel times: ObsPy TauP, first arrival among p/P/Pn/Pg (S: s/S/Sn/Sg) through the event's own
     model; model top at 1.75 km above sea level, station and event depths measured from it (station
     elevations kept). Picks without an arrival are dropped (230 of 903,704).
  5. Pick errors (recipe "local" of hypogt.synthetic.picks): Laplacian, scale 0.05 s (P) / 0.1 s (S);
     1% of P and 4% of S picks off by an extra 0.4-1.0 s / 0.4-1.4 s (either sign).
  6. UTDQuake pipeline as for every network: event QC, station distances/azimuths, pick QC, tables,
     travel-time model and z-scores, stats and figures. Events kept: the first 21,000 with >= 4 picks
     on >= 3 stations (of 21,300 drawn).

KNOWN LIMITS
  - 1D models only (no lateral changes); first arrivals only.
  - Magnitudes and the station/phase pattern are those of the nearest real event, not of the synthetic
    location; uniform events far from the seismicity copy an event up to ~61 km away (median 0.9 km).
  - Origin times are artificial.

FILES
  Per-event true velocity models and true travel times: work/models.parquet, work/picks_true.parquet
  (datasets/tx_syn). Event ids txsyn_00000 ... ; pick agency "synthetic (TauP, DB1D Monte Carlo)".
